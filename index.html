Temples of Lord Subramanya

❖
01

Tiruchendur Temple

Thoothukudi, Tamil Nadu

A sacred coastal temple where Lord Subramanya is worshipped as the victor over Surapadman. It symbolizes courage, protection and the triumph of good over evil.

It is believed that the divine war fought here by Lord Subramanya ended the forces of negativity and established dharma. Devotees pray here for victory over enemies, removal of obstacles and fearless progress in life.
02

Thiruparankundram Temple

Madurai, Tamil Nadu

An ancient and historic temple, believed to be the divine wedding place of Lord Subramanya. It brings harmony, removes obstacles and blesses devotees with strength.

The temple's spiritual vibrations are known to balance relationships, resolve conflicts and bless devotees with divine grace, courage and inner strength to overcome challenges.
03

Thiruthani Temple

Tiruvallur, Tamil Nadu

A serene hill temple where Lord Subramanya is worshipped for peace and relief from challenges. It fills life with confidence, clarity and positive energy.

It is believed that after the victory over Surapadman, Lord Subramanya came here to attain peace. Devotees visit for mental peace, emotional balance and to gain confidence for a new beginning.
04

Palani Temple

Dindigul, Tamil Nadu

The abode of wisdom and simplicity. Lord Subramanya here is worshipped as Dhandayudhapani, granting devotees spiritual growth, discipline and inner strength.

Known for its message of simplicity and detachment, Palani blesses devotees with wisdom, self-control and the strength to face life with patience and faith.
